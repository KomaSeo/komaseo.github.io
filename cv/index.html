Professional Summary

I’m working on positive computing and digital wellbeing, asking how people can retain agency over digital media and AI that shape their behavior and cognition. My current focus is helping people (1) examine how their digital and AI use affects them, (2) share these effects with the people around them, and (3) recommend harm prevention tools that fit their situation.
